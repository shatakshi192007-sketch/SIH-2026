# SIH 2026 Project 
🌊 Project Background

Floods can rapidly transform vulnerable areas into high-risk zones, putting lives, infrastructure, and essential resources at risk. One of the major challenges during flood situations is identifying hazardous zones, assessing their carrying capacity, and determining which areas require immediate relocation or evacuation.

Traditional assessment methods can be time-consuming and may not provide sufficiently dynamic information for rapidly changing situations. This creates a need for a technology-driven system that can combine environmental, geographical, and population-related data to support faster and more informed decisions.

📌 Project Description

Our project focuses on the intelligent identification of hazardous flood/red zones, assessment of the carrying capacity of affected areas, and identification of immediate relocation requirements for vulnerable populations.

The proposed system aims to analyze relevant data and generate actionable insights that can help authorities and disaster-management teams:

- Identify areas with high flood-related risk.
- Classify and visualize hazardous/red zones.
- Assess the carrying capacity and vulnerability of affected locations.
- Identify areas where population relocation may be urgently required.
- Prioritize vulnerable populations and high-risk regions.
- Support faster and more informed disaster-response planning.
- Improve resource allocation during emergency situations.

🎯 Objective

The primary objective is to develop a data-driven decision-support system that can help transform complex flood-related information into understandable risk indicators and actionable recommendations for emergency response and relocation planning.

💡 Why This Project?

Flood management is not only about detecting where water is present. Effective disaster response also requires understanding who is at risk, how vulnerable an area is, whether it can safely accommodate people, and where immediate relocation may be necessary.

Our approach attempts to bring these factors together into a single technology-based solution, making flood-risk assessment and emergency planning more systematic and efficient.

🚀 Project Vision

The long-term vision is to create a scalable system that can assist disaster-management authorities in moving from reactive response to proactive risk assessment, enabling earlier identification of vulnerable zones and better-informed evacuation and relocation decisions.
